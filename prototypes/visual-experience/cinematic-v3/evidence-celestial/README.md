## Native capture interpretation

Exploration receipts retain launch HEAD, source and built-asset hashes, actual PNG hashes, capture dimensions, resource poses, selected study, quality, Vela state and presentation-phase override. An earlier HEAD with working-tree changes is not evidence that those new sources were committed at that HEAD. Source/build hashes identify the captured candidate.

A/B/C study captures use the same native viewport, camera, resources and Balanced quality with environment phase held at zero. Shadow Reveal also has held phases 0, 30 and 60 seconds. These are explicitly scripted presentation comparisons under the study's fixed lights. Vela has a separate, unheld animation clock. Held-phase stills do not prove real-time timing, motion comfort or autonomous performance.

Files labeled `native-crop` are crops of the original native PNG pixels. Their receipts identify the source PNG/hash and crop rectangle; they are not independent renders or enlarged geometry detail. Full native stills retain their original dimensions. Raw motion-frame sequences are separately labeled when downscaled to fit the retention budget.

The review questions in each receipt remain **requires-human-review**. In particular, star visibility, brightness distribution, opaque-shell occlusion, structural credibility, light-driven revelation, focus readability, restraint and wonder cannot pass merely because a controller changed state. Reviewed structural crops show real cavities and bracing; that observation does not establish blanket occlusion correctness or photorealism.

## Performance and video integrity

[The celestial performance report](../PERFORMANCE-CELESTIAL-V3.5.md) is separate from older V3.5 performance history. Final workload values must come from the actual completed celestial receipt. Renderer totals combine the sky, separately lit architecture and foreground passes, including shadow rendering. Catalog-instance and architectural estimates remain separate from those submitted totals. Callback/submission intervals and encoder rate are not presented application FPS. Process working-set sums are not unique resident memory or VRAM; a short fresh-process probe cannot establish leak freedom.

[The sanitized video manifest](native-video.json) binds the actual a01 ambient and walkthrough WebMs to their same-basename native receipts and independent ffmpeg packet/frame inspections. The ambient video records 30.0025 seconds of recorder wall time and a 31.762-second last-packet PTS; the walkthrough records 27.1592 seconds of wall time and a 28.876-second last-packet PTS. Container duration is absent in both; timestamp differences are retained, without retiming or an FPS inference. All capture-binding checks passed and presentation overrides were null.

The unchanged decoded [early ambient frame](ambient-before.png) is at actual PTS `.313`, and the [late ambient frame](ambient-after.png) is at `26.532`. They show bay translation under the actual clock. These two pixels-only observations support visible motion, not a general material, occlusion or beauty pass. The full ambient clock advanced 30.0071 seconds and its natural angle `.07201704` radians (about 4.13 degrees).

Video review must use the **exact new per-video JSON/WebM pair**, with the video basename and SHA-256 bound to independent ffmpeg inspection. The preserved `.runtime/recording-latest.json` belongs to V3 and is not a pointer to new recordings. A previous review mistakenly selected that old receipt; Electron correctly decoded the older input. No inspector cache, decoder or native-window capture fault was demonstrated. New celestial files use `aerynth-v3-5-celestial-{ambient|native}-<timestamp>.webm` and a JSON file with the same basename.

The independent inspector records actual encoded packet PTS, decoded frame PTS and their relationship to requested seeks. Native recorder wall duration, missing container duration and encoded timestamp span remain distinct. Any discrepancy must remain explicit. Walkthroughs label scripted pointer events as rendered parallax demonstrations, not physical mouse latency. Ambient recordings release presentation overrides and show the actual permitted environment clock.

## Retention and local storage

All earlier passes remain intact, including rejected designs and failed review/recording attempts documented in the [preceding evidence index](../evidence-v3-5/README.md). Raw native motion PNGs stay local under ignored `evidence-celestial/**/raw/`; ignored files have not been deleted. Videos, decoding frames, profiles and operational receipts stay under ignored `.runtime/`. Public summaries must omit machine paths, process/window IDs and unrelated application data while retaining hashes, timing limits, source identity and capture-binding truth.

ReserveGuard preserves separate 30 GiB reserves on C: and D:. Each native pass admits up to 256 MiB including its 190 MiB PNG cap and profile/report headroom. The wider task allowance and rechecks cover other bounded writers. This is an instruction and monitoring policy, not an OS quota; it cannot stop unrelated apps, cloud tasks or processes that did not load the rule. No unrelated data or retained evidence was deleted to satisfy it.

Owner artistic acceptance remains outstanding. Publication of this evidence is not a production merge or a declaration that the complete artistic standard has been achieved.
