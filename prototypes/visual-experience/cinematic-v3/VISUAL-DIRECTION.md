## Three runnable studies

These are actual shader/geometry variants in the same native executable, not generated concept art or palette swaps. Captures are `evidence/study-*.png`; earlier actual captures remain under `evidence/iteration-1/`.

| Study | Spatial/material idea | Strength | Cost and risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Luminous Architecture | Cropped beveled solid aperture, graded horizon, selective grazing light, dark suspended folios, folded Vela | A stable sense of place and the calmest text hierarchy | Extruded surfaces and reflection environment; can become a dark background graphic if the light is too restrained |
| Living Intelligence | Two recessed procedural saddle sheets; contours bend toward input and state | Strongest visible environmental participation, genuine non-flat field | Large fragment coverage; luminous contours can compete with reading or resemble a screensaver |
| Aetheric Observatory | Tilted orbital instrument, recessed meridian, sparse stations | Clear relational space, precision and distance | Too easily becomes familiar sci-fi instrumentation; line aliasing needs review |

**Provisional choice: Luminous Architecture.** It best accommodates everyday writing and recognizable object locations. This is an experimental choice, not acceptance. The other studies remain usable for direct comparison. Avoid blending all three visual grammars.

Subsequent direct review favored the Living Intelligence field's response to moving resources, as well as the pointer-responsive presence. The updated review command therefore opens **Living Intelligence**. All three studies remain available; this does not establish an approved production direction. The owner also requested relatively strong independent ambient Vela motion, now the Expressive default.

## Geometry and compositional revisions

The first actual capture exposed a flat dark arch, a uniformly gray floor, and intersecting ribbon shards. The second pass replaced the arch with beveled solids, replaced the reflective gray floor with a fogged procedural plane, and rebuilt Vela as a tapered strip. Its actual capture then exposed an unfinished C-shaped silhouette and excessive darkness.

The third pass makes Vela a closed asymmetric double-loop with a physically separated over/under crossing and a narrow companion following the same surface. It shifts the aperture away from her silhouette, adds selective inner bevel light and a graded distant atmosphere. These are geometry/material/composition changes. Earlier captures and the final evaluation record the remaining weaknesses.

Further direct capture review removed the companion, softened unselected polish, added pose-registered GPU backing/bevel/contact layers, separated the reading folio/circular material lens/slim note, and replaced peripheral overlap with interactive spines. Status/preview clipping was corrected. Strong independent ambient Vela motion was then added without changing the recognized underlying identity or the card-driven field response.

## Material and typography

The scene uses near-black depth, neutral ink, quiet lavender edge illumination, and rare pale pearl highlights. Folios carry a thin rim, shadow, layered lower edge and pointer-aware highlight derived from the same pose as their elevation. A note is warmer and shallower; research is denser; the expanded working plane approaches opacity. No blur is needed to read text. Background geometry is genuinely three-dimensional; folio refraction is an approximation, not a physically based optical claim.

System fonts keep the prototype offline and crisp. Small metadata is intentionally quiet, but still needs formal contrast and assistive-technology review. Dense text is 16px in the expanded plane, selectable/editable, and never painted into WebGL. Quality tiers retain hierarchy and silhouette.

## Technology research and choice

Canvas 2D/CSS from the earlier candidates cannot itself establish sculptural occlusion or real surface normals. Raw WebGL would offer control at higher maintenance cost. Three.js provides geometry, camera, materials, shader integration, LOD, and context tools; a DOM/GPU hybrid preserves practical input and accessibility. WebGPU and React Three Fiber remain alternatives, not rejected technologies; neither is required to test this spatial/material hypothesis. There is no quantitative comparative GPU/API winner from this experiment.

Electron is a temporary measurement/launch adapter because a working toolchain existed and its native capture/process APIs make the experiment reproducible. Version38.4.0 crashed in the Windows accessibility layer during expansion. Version45.2.1 was then verified against the official release registry and used with accessibility enabled. This resolves the observed path in the later run; it is not proof of the exact upstream cause or universal stability. Tauri equivalence, startup/memory tradeoffs, OLED/HDR composition and final shell selection remain unproven.

Primary technical references: [Three.js documentation](https://threejs.org/docs/), [Electron security guidance](https://www.electronjs.org/docs/latest/tutorial/security), [Electron release registry](https://releases.electronjs.org/). Performance claims rely on local evidence, not these references.


## Owner addendum refinement

The next pass preserves the violet field, folded silhouette, distinct resource roles and restrained material family. It concentrates on the flagship workspace → focused conversation → local/synthetic Vela involvement → workspace journey. `scene-state.ts` derives one active resource anchor, focus, attention, interaction salience and permitted ambient gain. Real mouse input remains separate and additive. No performance pressure or hidden agent state is invented.

The rejected vertical spines become horizontal resource anchors: a small optical lens and a warmer notebook with readable labels; compact windows expose labels on hover or keyboard focus. The same resource articles interpolate into those anchors. The conversation gains a selectable scripted message stream, a separate composer, bounded persistent local messages and related-resource/Vela actions. These demonstrate useful ordinary working content without pretending a model is connected.

The integrated frame uses Electron's supported hidden-titlebar/native-controls overlay, a dark caption strip and a declared drag region. Native minimize/maximize/restore/close controls remain OS-owned. [Electron's primary guide](https://www.electronjs.org/docs/latest/tutorial/custom-title-bar) documents this configuration. Mixed-DPI, Windows Snap hover behavior, display removal and full accessibility remain qualification work.
