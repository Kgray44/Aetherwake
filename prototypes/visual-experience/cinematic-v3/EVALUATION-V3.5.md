## Owner corrections and actual-result review

Two subsequent owner corrections sharpened the initial implementation:

1. **Make the structure substantially larger.** The primary concept now uses a radius of 21.5 scene units, with its center farther outside the view. The visible portion is a broad, gently curving arc continuing beyond both the top and bottom edges. This restores the intended impression that the environment is only a small view into a much larger constructed world.
2. **Develop its surface and ambient light behavior.** Finer inset panels, recessed detail, a spatially varying optical veil and slow physical rotation let different structural regions emerge and recede under fixed scene lighting. This is an original procedural optical approximation, not volumetric transport or an agent-activity signal.

The independently decoded local ambient frames at `.runtime/v3-5/independent-ambient-review/ambient-0.3.png` and `ambient-26.5.png` were reviewed directly. The earlier frame reveals more upper structure; the later frame darkens the upper region while revealing more lower structure. Vela's pose also changes without an interaction journey. This supplies concrete evidence that the architectural presence and ribbon are not static compositions.

Those frames belong to the historical native ambient recording `.runtime/aerynth-v3-5-ambient-1793565278764.webm`. Its receipt records approximately 30 seconds of recorder wall time, but encoded presentation timestamps and native wall time need not be identical. The requested 30 fps is not application presentation FPS. Its launch receipt carries older Git HEAD alongside modified V3.5 hashes; it must not be relabeled as an exact-commit `877a6d2` recording. Later exact-source receipts remain separate. The contradictory Electron inspection PNGs under `.runtime/v3-5/video-review-1793565328765/` were excluded: the review command had read the preserved V3 `recording-latest.json` and supplied an older video. Independent ffmpeg decoding of the correct unique V3.5 filenames confirmed the recordings were correct. This was a wrong-input review error, not evidence of a capture, cache or decoder defect.

The larger arc now has credible section depth, layered surfaces and partial visibility. It is a clearer celestial architectural anchor than the V3 curve. Its broad shell can still read as a mechanical rim, and some regions remain fairly uniform under the current lighting. More detail is not automatically a better composition. The owner's judgment is needed on whether the latest balance conveys an immense observatory rather than foreground machinery.

## Remaining boundaries and next review

Preserve the double-loop Vela, expressive autonomous motion, additive pointer response, reactive Living surface, shared coordinator, persistent resources, type-specific materials and opaque reading surfaces. The latest architecture should be evaluated in motion before adding further objects or ornament.

The next owner review should assess whether the much larger partial arc conveys celestial scale; whether the upper/lower reveal and slow rotation feel natural; whether Vela's crossings remain clean during motion; whether the softer Living field is preferable to V3's finer contours; and whether resources now feel like distinct materials within the same environment. A sustained reading/editing session should evaluate expressive Vela and the changing architectural light separately from a short cinematic demonstration.

Presented application timing, an actual physical 60 Hz comparison, attributable VRAM, application energy/thermal behavior, prolonged memory stability, mixed-DPI/display-disconnect behavior, screen-reader acceptance, formal text contrast and calibrated SDR/OLED/HDR behavior remain unqualified unless later evidence explicitly closes them. Available WPR profiles or GPU tool discovery do not supply these measurements; see [measurement-tool findings](evidence-v3-5/performance-tools.json) and the separate performance report. Real Codex/Vela integration, production security and the complete application architecture are not established by this prototype.

That is monitoring of agent-owned work, not an OS quota: unrelated applications, cloud tasks and already-running processes that did not load the rule remain outside its enforcement coverage.
