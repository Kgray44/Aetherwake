## 1. What has been approved

The definitive mark is a sculptural, flowing **A** formed from broad, softly folded, interrelated translucent ribbons. Its visual language is deep indigo, electric blue, soft violet, and lavender-white reflection, suspended against a quiet near-black field.

The design was selected specifically because it expresses **Liquid Intelligence** through fluid continuity, visual restraint, and an immediately recognizable silhouette. It retains organic curves and broad uninterrupted surfaces instead of dense outlining, circuitry, excessive seams, or decorative complexity.

This is an explicit **visual identity selection**, not a statement that the application, logo animation, raster derivatives, legal clearance, or brand registration has been completed.

## 2. Distinctions and intended use

- **Aetherwake** is the overall development project.
- **Aerynth** is the desktop application and owns this mark.
- **Vela** is a distinct assistant presence. Do not simply reuse the Aerynth mark as her avatar.
- **Liquid Intelligence** is the unified experience language, not a separate product logo.

Intended placements include the application launch sequence, splash/about screens, repository presentation, app icon family, and branded documentation. The original cinematic mark may be rendered in full fidelity where space allows. Taskbar, tray, and favicon use require separate, deliberately simplified designs that preserve the approved silhouette.

## 3. Visual invariants

1. Preserve the **flowing A silhouette**, the central negative-space opening, and the soft folded-ribbon character.
2. Preserve the **restraint**: large calm surfaces, selective highlights, controlled glow, and a strong light/dark hierarchy.
3. Favor softly integrated material transitions over harsh extra lines or ornamental paneling.
4. Keep the mark distinct from Vela's visual embodiment and from transient UI state indicators.
5. Do not claim that a mockup, traced vector, regenerated variant, compressed derivative, or transparent cutout is the approved master without explicit owner review.
6. Do not remove or fake the opaque original background while labeling the result as a transparent original.
7. Respect reduced-motion and performance constraints for animated presentations; a static rendering must remain recognizable.

These are owner-approved **design intentions**, not a new numbered governance charter. Applicable published and accepted governance remains authoritative.

## 4. Asset handling and derivatives

- The **exact supplied master PNG** is committed at `assets/brand/aerynth-mark-master.png`, with source bytes unmodified. The recorded SHA-256 and matching Git blob identity establish the checked-in source.
- The master has an **opaque background**; naming its file `*.png` does not imply transparency.
- Any future `transparent`, `monochrome`, `icon-256`, `svg`, `animated`, or other variant must have a distinct filename and must identify its parent source.
- Derived files should state how they were produced, intended use, scale limits, and whether they have been reviewed and approved.
- Do not replace the master with a newer AI regeneration simply because it appears higher-resolution or more detailed. Past iterations showed that added seams can reduce logo clarity.
- A full-resolution 3D/vector reconstruction requires actual design and validation; the approved PNG alone is not proof such source files exist.

## 5. Experience integration

The launch experience described in [Aerynth Experience Vision](../vision/AERYNTH-EXPERIENCE-VISION.md) remains the creative basis: subtle light gathers, the **Aerynth mark** resolves with precision, and **Vela** appears nearby as a separate presence. Motion should reveal the inherent ribbon structure, never conceal a different shape under showy effects.

There is no requirement to make this master image spin, ripple, or breathe continuously. Animation should be carefully authored, state-meaningful where applicable, and respectful of attention and resource budgets.
