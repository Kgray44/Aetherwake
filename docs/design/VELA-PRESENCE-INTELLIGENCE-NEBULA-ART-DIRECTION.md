> **Creative north star:** Looking at Vela Presence should feel like standing before a vast, intelligent, living astronomical phenomenon. It is not a diagram of reasoning, a spinning emblem, a 2D smoke sprite, a particle-screen saver, or a radial menu. Her central nebula reaches into neighboring cognitive and operational nebulas through continuous, deforming, luminous matter. The user can watch real activity without sacrificing truth, control, or usability.

## 1. What the reference actually looks like

The provided reference is indispensable to artistic acceptance. Its salient properties, which reviewers must compare side-by-side against the actual submitted image, are:

1. **Asymmetrical open silhouette:** an irregular airborne mass with sweeping, twisting, translucent veils. It is *not* circular, spherical, a tidy torus, a brain icon, or a compact glowing blob.
2. **Silk-like transparency with depth:** multiple superimposed gossamer sheets, thin refractive-looking folds, soft opaque luminous knots, and regions of almost total transparency. Outer boundaries are delicate, torn, curling, and wispy, not thick hard edges.
3. **Interleaved chromatic body:** silver-white luminous highlights, icy blue/cyan strata, lavender and violet folds, subtle pink/pearl areas, and deep ultramarine pockets. Color lives *inside* the object rather than as flat gradient coloring on the surface.
4. **Interior luminosity:** bright, cloudlike star fields and fine sparkling concentrations appear at several depths behind translucent material. Light seems to move through the body and illuminate folds from within.
5. **Distinct scale of structures:** large drifting sheets; medium curling seams and ribbons; fine hairlike lightning/filament paths; micro-particles and stars. No single frequency dominates.
6. **Beautiful negative space:** large regions of near-black between transparent wisps, open gaps, overlapping folds, and a quiet, dark surrounding field.
7. **Directionality without mechanical symmetry:** upper-reaching stretches, sweeping diagonal flow, an elegant lower curling return, areas of tension and areas of calm. The whole reads as connected, living substance.
8. **Atmospheric rather than sharp mesh appearance:** translucency, optical depth, subtle scattering, anisotropic highlights and uneven density, never a plain wireframe or a flat sprite.

The implementation must **materially resemble this supplied reference** in silhouette family, translucency, nested structure, internal illumination, and overall beauty. A different color-matched blob is not close enough. This is a perceptual target rather than a license to project the original 2D image onto a plane.

### 1.1 Look-alike failure examples

A radial galaxy with six round clouds; a WebGL sphere with vertex noise; a smooth ball of smoke; a static transparent PNG with parallax; a single shader plane emitting rays; a light-blue jellyfish; an orbiting collection of glass cards; lines drawn from a logo to six labels; and a neon dashboard are all **rejected** as the defining experience, however attractive they might look as isolated experiments.

### 1.2 Artwork custody

The user's attachment is a visual reference, not automatic confirmation of public-repository redistribution rights. Keep the original in the approved design-review evidence store, with source and permissions recorded. Do not silently scrape a replacement image or check an unlicensed copy into the public repo. Design and acceptance artifacts must refer to the exact reviewed reference, not an arbitrary image search result.

## 2. Global composition and dramaturgy

The central **Intelligence Nebula** is Vela's global, symbolic orchestration presence. It occupies a meaningful volume in a genuine 3D scene, never a fixed rectangle. Around it are six recognizable but generous **domain nebulae**: Planning; Execution; Writing/Creation; Communication/Streaming; Memory/Context; Analysis/Observation. The classification is a default semantic mapping, not permission to infer invisible reasoning.

The six should not resemble evenly spaced menu buttons. Their broad spatial homes are stable for learned orientation, while their density, volume, local filaments and relative drift evolve organically. Some can be partly off-camera or further away, provided all remain navigable. A domain is not simply an icon with a smoke effect; it is a substantial three-dimensional family of living luminous matter, related visually to the central body but differentiated by turbulence, filament behavior, internal structure and activity.

The central scene should read beautifully at rest. Users should be able to admire it without task activity. Silence must be elegant, not fake computation.

## 3. Morphological construction

**Macro form:** multiple large drifting, stretching, rejoining veils of light, composed as deformable translucent volumes/surfaces in 3D. The entire volume folds, unfurls, draws inward, and opens asymmetrically; the camera sees different overlapping structures at different angles.

**Meso structure:** sweeping internal ribbons and arc-shaped filaments that run through, around and beneath the major veils. Seams can temporarily brighten, branch, braid and disappear into depth. Tension along a veil should produce related deformation of neighboring layers.

**Micro structure:** embedded drifting particles, light grains, star-like pinpoints, local turbulence and wispy detail. They move in coherent 3D flow fields and are not merely random screen-space sparkles.

**Lighting:** some regions are backlit through multiple sheets, some are lit from internal knots, some glow on their edges at grazing angles. Variation in extinction and emission reveals real layering. Bright regions must never flatten all detail into a white rectangle.

**Identity:** dynamic yet recognizable. No periodic 5-second shape reset, no random reseeding that teleports the silhouette, and no mode change that swaps Vela for a completely different object. Broad form motifs persist through change.

## 4. The defining behavior: living extensions

When a domain becomes active, the core **changes shape toward it**. A stretch begins as a distributed deformation of a region of the central cloud, not a detached beam. The region pulls outward, fibers align along the direction, transparent layers lengthen, inner currents intensify, and one or more tendrils bridge the space. The domain responds by opening and glowing where the flow arrives.

A convincing extension includes:
- a tension buildup and deformation at its origin;
- connected material and volume during emergence, travel, and return;
- several intertwined scales of vapor, surface, filament and particles;
- continuous transport of light along the extension, with phase lag between deformation and transmitted pulses;
- splintered hairline wisps at its boundary and occasional rejoining into wider translucent folds;
- graceful branching to simultaneous domains and collision/occlusion awareness;
- response to sudden cancellation or failure without snapping, pretending success, or dumping particles;
- relaxation and reabsorption after the real operation goes idle, without abrupt geometry disappearance.

At least two concurrent extensions must be visually independent, yet derive from the same core field. Where domain links exist solely to show broad topical engagement, they must not be misrepresented as verified causal data transfers.

### 4.1 Motion signatures by domain

| Domain | Motion family | Event interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Planning | Converging layered streams, unfolding structural arcs | Visible plan creation or revision; not unobservable private thoughts |
| Execution | Directed currents, branching/rejoining pulses | Actual tool runs, scheduled work and verified result events |
| Writing / Creation | Fine ordered ribbons and materialization ripples | Real output/diff/token or document events when available |
| Communication / Streaming | Radiating fine pulses, outward and inward exchanges | Sent/received message or streaming events actually observed |
| Memory / Context | Deep layered, slower sedimented currents | Authorized context retrieval, loading and memory updates |
| Analysis / Observation | Responsive scanning and overlapping luminous folds | Document analysis, measurement, inspection and source processing |

A single task may activate several domains. They are composable, not mutually exclusive animation presets.

## 5. Action motes and satellites

Small contextual manifestations appear near the domain or tendril corresponding to active work: a file read, command run, agent dispatch, source fetch, response, verification, human approval, or uncertainty. They float in physically coherent space with stabilized positioning. They are **not** a ring of UI cards, and not every raw event warrants a new mote.

A satellite may expose a short label, recognizable icon or miniature live preview on focus. The orbital layout uses damped spring movement, gentle field drift, overlap avoidance and stable anchors. Completed activities dissipate or rejoin the main flow, but remain accessible through a recent-activity Wake and the canonical trace.

An activity linked to a project can be selected and used as a spatial entry into that project's Intelligence Stream. A user can inspect provenance without changing work state.

## 6. The essential cinematic transition

The user selects an Aetherwake activity in Vela Presence. Its domain brightens. Related tendrils direct the scene's energy toward it. The camera follows the selected luminous connection through the volumetric environment. Its particles resolve into pathways; those pathways become the *actual plan/execution graph* of Aetherwake. The project Intelligence Stream expands into navigable focus and the Activity Theater becomes active.

Reverse travel contracts the project graph into a connected luminous formation and pulls the camera back through the same route into the global nebular field. Switching directly between two project streams follows a shared spatial environment, not an unrelated page swap.

**One continuous spatial universe. No disconnected screen changes.**

Continuous *perceived* travel can be rendered using coordinated scene transitions across different renderers; it does not require one monolithic GPU world. Selection, project identity, operation status, camera focus and task continuity must survive throughout.

## 7. User experience and control

- User camera controls, orbit/pan/zoom and focus-lock must take priority over auto-framing.
- A reduced-motion/non-GPU mode must preserve information, navigation, approvals and work.
- View transitions never start, suspend, abort or duplicate execution.
- Hover-only access is insufficient: keyboard focus and alternative lists must make actions accessible.
- If the system has partial observability, show that fact instead of implying that Vela's whole private mental state is exposed.
- External progress is sourced, not invented. The nebular metaphor is explicitly symbolic.
- Multiple active projects can appear together without disclosing unrelated private data outside authorization.
- Visual intensity is calm during focused typing, approvals and dense reading.

## 8. Artistic acceptance scenes

1. **Idle hero shot:** reference-matched translucent asymmetrical nebula; multiple visible interior planes, internal star fields and negative space; camera movement demonstrates real parallax.
2. **Close orbit:** camera moves noticeably around the central core; folds and internal density resolve at different depths. The silhouette changes believably, not via a pasted image.
3. **First extension:** Planning becomes active and a broad, wispy, connected region stretches from the core to its neighboring nebula. It visibly *belongs* to the central mass.
4. **Parallel work:** Planning, Execution, and Writing engage at once. Three independently flowing but compositionally balanced extensions form; actions populate and reorder without hopping.
5. **Cancellation:** one operation becomes unknown/blocked/failed; its movement communicates the true state without implying completion or abruptly snapping back.
6. **Project travel:** select a project satellite, traverse its tendril, resolve into a true project Stream graph; reverse successfully with identical source objects.
7. **Long idle and long load:** no obvious short loop, unbounded drift, resource leak or loss of legibility.
8. **Reduced graphics:** the same state and controls remain inspectable with no decorative rendering dependency.

Every hero scene requires actual GPU video evidence, controlled camera movement, and reference-side-by-side human visual review. A still screenshot or an attractive shader sample is not product acceptance.

## 9. Non-goals, corrections, and release posture

Vela Presence does not disclose private chain-of-thought, classify a person's emotions, or represent fictitious neural activity. It does not replace the project Intelligence Stream, the Timeline, an approval control or a terminal. The user's reference is a target for visual-material fidelity, not a guarantee of physically accurate astrophysics.

Approval of this design direction is **not approval of any initial implementation that merely resembles it superficially**. Rendering, motion, navigation, actual data integration and accessibility each have independent acceptance gates. See the accompanying technical contracts and review plan.
